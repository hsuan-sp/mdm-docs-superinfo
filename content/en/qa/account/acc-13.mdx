**In a standard Apple School Manager (ASM) deployment, there is no global 'Self-Service Password Reset' toggle for students. Password authority is strictly retained by IT administrators.**

Due to international educational privacy regulations (like COPPA and GDPR-K), Managed Apple Accounts operate under significantly different security paradigms compared to consumer Apple Accounts. If you cannot find a toggle to allow user self-reset, this is by design. Password resolution follows these operational logic paths:

## 1. Federated Authentication (Google / Microsoft)

- If your ASM is federated with Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra ID, Apple’s native password management is overridden.
- **Resolution**: Password resets are **handled entirely within the Google or Microsoft portals**. Users simply update their identity provider password, and their Apple services sync the new credentials automatically. This is the _only_ official way to enable true "Self-Service Password Reset" in an educational setting.

## 2. Standard Managed Apple Accounts (Non-Federated)

- **Blocked Pathways**: Students cannot use the standard `iforgot.apple.com` portal. The system will reject the request outright (unless a school-controlled recovery phone number was explicitly bound to the account, which is rare in bulk deployments).
- **Required Admin Action**: Staff with "Administrator", "Site Manager", or "People Manager" privileges must manually log into ASM, locate the user, and select **[Create Sign-In Information]** or **[Reset Password]** to generate a new temporary password.

## 3. OS-Level Account Recovery (OS 26 Context)

While Apple optimized local credential caching and offline recovery flows in **iPadOS 26 and macOS 26 Tahoe**, the ultimate authority to issue a new password for a locked Managed Account remains centralized within the ASM console to prevent unauthorized takeovers.

## Strategy: Reducing Administrative Overhead

The daily burden of resetting student passwords can consume significant IT resources. To mitigate this:

1. **Batch Generation**: At the start of each semester, utilize ASM's bulk-selection tool to generate PDFs or CSVs containing temporary passwords for entire incoming classes, delegating distribution to homeroom teachers.
2. **Implement Federation**: We strongly advise deploying Federated Authentication. Consolidating into a single identity provider (like Google) allows students to use credentials they already know, eliminating the administrative nightmare of maintaining dual password systems.
