**Once a school verifies its official domain in Apple School Manager (ASM), the system automatically scans for and identifies all 'Personal Apple Accounts' registered with that school email address, triggering a standardized migration process.**

To protect user privacy while enforcing organizational security, Apple employs a protocol known as "Domain Capture" to resolve historical mixtures of personal and professional accounts.

## The Conflict Resolution Workflow:

1. **System Identification**: Upon verification of your domain (e.g., `@school.edu.tw`) in ASM, Apple’s servers scan their global database to locate any consumer Apple Accounts currently occupying that domain space.
2. **Automated Notification**: Apple dispatches official emails and device push notifications to these users, alerting them that the organization has claimed ownership of the domain.
3. **60-Day Grace Period**: Users are granted 60 days to address the conflict:
   - **Change Personal Email**: The user logs in and updates their Apple Account ID to a private email address (e.g., Gmail). **All personal data** (iCloud backups, photos, App Store purchases) remains completely intact; only the login name changes.
   - **Release for Organization**: Once the user vacates the school email address, the IT department can immediately claim it to provision a brand-new **Managed Apple Account**.
4. **Forced Release (Domain Capture)**: If a user ignores the warnings and takes no action after 60 days, Apple forcefully intervenes. Upon the user's next login attempt, their account is temporarily renamed (often to a `@gtempaccount.com` placeholder), and they are compelled to provide a new email. The school domain address is simultaneously released back to ASM for administrative use.

## ⚠️ Critical Privacy Assurance:

This process often causes anxiety among staff. You must clearly communicate: **School administrators "CANNOT" access any personal data (photos, messages, credit cards) through this process.** The organization is only reclaiming the rights to the "Account Name" (the email address itself), not capturing the underlying user content.

## Admin Dashboard Path:

1. Log in to [Apple School Manager](https://school.apple.com).
2. Navigate to **[Preferences]** > **[Directory Sync]** (or **User Sign-in**).
3. Select your verified domain in the **[Domains]** section to view a real-time list of conflict statuses and the remaining grace period days for affected accounts.

## Strategy: Domain Consolidation Preparation

Many schools will aim to implement Federated Authentication to streamline IT operations. However, federation cannot be enabled until all domain conflicts are resolved. We recommend issuing formal internal communications at least six months prior to the transition, explaining the "separation of personal and professional data" policy and allowing staff ample time to update their personal Apple Accounts.
