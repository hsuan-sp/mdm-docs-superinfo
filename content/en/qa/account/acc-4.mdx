**The VPP (Volume Purchase Program) Token is the secure cryptographic bridge that syncs app and book licenses between Apple School Manager (ASM) and Jamf Pro. It must be renewed annually.**

If the token expires, Jamf Pro will lose the ability to fetch new licenses or reclaim unused ones, effectively halting all app deployments across your campus.

## Renewal Workflow:

### Step 1: Download the Token from ASM

1. Log in to [school.apple.com](https://school.apple.com) using an account with "Administrator" or "Content Manager" privileges.
2. Click your **[Account Name]** in the bottom-left corner and select **[Preferences]**.
3. Select the **[Payments and Billing]** tab.
4. Locate the **[Apps and Books]** section and find your specific Location (e.g., "Main Campus").
5. Click **[Download]** next to the VPP Token for that location. A `.vpptoken` file will be saved to your local drive.

### Step 2: Upload the Token to Jamf Pro

1. Log in to your Jamf Pro dashboard.
2. Navigate to **[Settings]** > **[Global Management]** > **[Volume Purchasing]**.
3. Click on the corresponding Location entry and select **[Edit]**.
4. Click **[Upload]** and select the `.vpptoken` file you just downloaded.
5. Click **[Save]**. The status should return to "Active," with the expiration date extended by exactly one year.

## Troubleshooting Tips:

- **Location Mismatch**: If you manage multiple sites in ASM, ensure you upload the token that matches the location configured in Jamf Pro. Using the wrong token will cause your licensed app counts to incorrectly display as zero.
- **Force Sync**: After successfully renewing the token, it is highly recommended to click the **[Sync]** button in Jamf Pro to force an immediate update of your software inventory.

## Strategy:

Annual VPP token renewal is a core responsibility for IT staff. We recommend scheduling this task during the summer break as part of an "Annual IT Health Check" to ensure uninterrupted software delivery when the autumn semester begins.
