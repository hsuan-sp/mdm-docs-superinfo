**They are fundamentally different in system architecture. Jamf Pro is the actual 'Remote Control' (the active MDM system), while moemdm is the Ministry of Education's 'Dashboard' (a passive reporting platform).**

New IT coordinators often mistakenly believe they must duplicate their configuration efforts across both platforms. Understanding this structural division is critical to avoiding wasted administrative effort:

## 1. Jamf Pro (The School's Active Management Core)

- **Core Functionality**: This is your exclusive command center for all technical operations. Any action—such as deploying educational apps, pushing Wi-Fi certificates, wiping forgotten passcodes, or applying web restrictions—must be executed here.
- **Technical Flow**: Management payloads are dispatched directly from the Jamf server to the student iPads via Apple's secure APNs network.
- **Target Users**: IT Directors, system administrators, and authorized teaching staff.

## 2. moemdm (The MOE's Passive Reporting Platform)

- **Core Functionality**: A centralized web platform built by the MOE to audit the "Asset Health" and monitor the "Usage Rate KPIs" of hundreds of thousands of devices deployed under the Digital Initiative.
- **Technical Flow**: moemdm is strictly a data recipient. Changing settings within the moemdm web portal **will never** alter the physical behavior of an iPad.
- **The Integration**: moemdm servers periodically query your Jamf Pro via secure APIs to fetch inventory data. As long as your Jamf Pro inventory is properly organized, data flows to moemdm automatically for MOE compliance audits.

## Why did the government separate these systems?

- **Cybersecurity Isolation**: The MOE does not need—and from a security standpoint, should not possess—a "God Mode" capable of directly controlling every school device in Taiwan, as this creates a catastrophic single point of failure.
- **Pedagogical Autonomy**: By dividing the systems, schools retain 100% sovereign control over their classroom environments (via their local Jamf Pro instance), while the MOE achieves its necessary financial and asset auditing goals via moemdm.

## Strategy: Dual Platform Operations

To fix a broken iPad or deploy software, always log in to **Jamf Pro**. To verify if your school is meeting its semester usage KPIs, log in to **moemdm**.
Understanding this strategic division is key to foundational IT efficiency: **Active management happens in Jamf Pro; compliance reporting happens in moemdm.**
