**Both are Apple-validated, Apple-first MDM solutions, but they differ fundamentally in design philosophy, language support, and depth of technical capability.**

When a school must independently budget for and procure its own MDM licenses, understanding these differences is critical for IT directors planning their infrastructure.

## Core Specification Comparison:

| Feature                         | Jamf Pro (Flagship)                                                                                                                                                           | Jamf School (K-12 Focused)                                                                                                                                                     |
| :------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Localization**                | Offers comprehensive, official **Traditional Chinese** localization.                                                                                                          | Administrative interface **lacks Traditional Chinese**, supporting primarily English and European languages.                                                                   |
| **Technical Depth**             | Built on "Smart Groups" and "Policies." Supports custom Bash/zsh scripting and Extension Attributes, offering unparalleled deep-level automation and macOS management.        | Designed specifically for K-12 simplicity. Relies heavily on Configuration Profiles. Scripting and complex logical deployments are significantly limited compared to Jamf Pro. |
| **Scalability (Multi-Tenancy)** | Features a robust **"Sites"** architecture, allowing seamless segregation of departments or campuses within a single instance with granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). | Supports multi-school setups, but the depth of instance isolation and global resource sharing is more streamlined.                                                             |
| **API Capabilities**            | Boasts industry-leading Classic and Pro APIs, enabling deep, programmatic integrations with Azure, Google Workspace, and internal asset databases.                            | Offers functional APIs, but with a narrower scope of readable/writable endpoints.                                                                                              |
| **Companion Apps**              | Offers Jamf Teacher to supplement Apple Classroom.                                                                                                                            | A core selling point is its deep, intuitive integration with the proprietary Jamf Teacher and Jamf Parent applications.                                                        |

## Strategy: MDM License Procurement Guide

- **Stay with Jamf Pro**: If your IT staff relies on the **Traditional Chinese interface**, utilizes complex "Smart Groups" for dynamic device filtering, or plans to manage Mac fleets requiring automated script execution (e.g., printer driver installations), you must budget to renew Jamf Pro.
- **Migrate to Jamf School**: If your deployment is small, staff are comfortable navigating an English-only console, and your needs are restricted to basic app deployment and simple restrictions, Jamf School may offer a more streamlined alternative.
