**Announced at WWDC 2025, the 'Restrict Apple Account to Managed Only' feature is a flagship security update for educational environments. It enforces that organizational devices can ONLY be signed into using Managed Apple Accounts, permanently closing the loophole of mixed personal/professional device usage.**

Historically, while MDMs could restrict app installations, students could still navigate to "Settings" and sign in with their personal Apple Accounts. This resulted in personal iCloud photos, iMessages, and consumer app purchases cluttering school-owned hardware, causing significant privacy and management headaches.
This new feature intercepts login attempts at the operating system’s lowest level (independent of MDM profiles), providing the ultimate Data Loss Prevention (DLP) and data segregation.

## System-Level Configuration (via ASM)

This is a global organizational security policy. It must be enabled by a user holding the **[Administrator]** role:

1. Log in to **[Apple School Manager (ASM)]**.
2. Navigate to **[Access Management]** in the sidebar.
3. Select the **[Apple Services]** tab.
4. Locate the option titled **[Apple Account on Organization Devices]**.
5. Toggle the setting to **[Allow Only Managed Apple Accounts to Sign In]**.
6. Save the settings. Once saved, the policy propagates immediately to all eligible enrolled devices.

## Technical Characteristics and Strict Limitations

- **Low-Level Enforcement**: The restriction operates during the initial "Setup Assistant" phase and continuously within "System Settings." If a user attempts to sign in with a consumer Apple ID (e.g., `@gmail.com`), the OS immediately blocks the attempt with an error prompt.
- **OS Version Threshold**: Devices must be updated to **iOS 26, iPadOS 26, macOS 26 (Tahoe)**, or later to support this low-level interception.
- **Device Eligibility**: The device must be recognized as "Organization Owned"—meaning it was procured via an authorized reseller and resides in ASM (Automated Device Enrollment and Supervised Mode are highly recommended). Teacher-owned BYOD devices are exempt from this policy.

## Strategy: Asset Ownership Preparation

When device ownership fully transfers to the school:

- **Strong Recommendation**: To ensure these devices remain strictly dedicated to educational purposes for the remainder of their lifecycle, IT Directors should enable this feature globally. This permanently prevents students from treating school iPads as personal entertainment devices.
- **Prerequisites**: Before activating this lockdown, you must ensure that your school has a fully operational Federated Authentication (Google/Entra ID) pipeline so that every student and teacher has a valid Managed Apple Account ready to use; otherwise, users will be completely locked out of the devices.
