**Yes. This process is called "Federated Authentication." It allows students and staff to use their existing school credentials to automatically create and log into Managed Apple Accounts.**

By linking Apple School Manager (ASM) with your Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD), you establish a seamless "Single Sign-On" (SSO) experience across your entire device fleet.

## Key Benefits:

- **Unified Passwords**: Users do not need to memorize a separate password for Apple services. They use their primary campus login for iCloud, Classroom, and Shared iPads.
- **Automated Provisioning (SCIM)**: When you create or delete a student account in your Google or Microsoft admin console, the corresponding Managed Apple Account in ASM is automatically created or deactivated—eliminating manual CSV uploads.
- **Centralized Security**: Password complexity rules and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) policies are enforced by your primary identity provider.
- **Platform SSO for Mac**: On macOS 15 and later (including the current **macOS 26**), Platform SSO allows teachers to unlock their Mac and simultaneously authenticate to all underlying Apple services using just their Microsoft or Google credentials.

## Technical Requirements (Admin Workflow):

1. **Domain Verification**: You must prove ownership of your school's email domain (e.g., `@school.edu.tw`) within ASM by publishing a specific TXT record to your public DNS.
2. **IdP Integration**: An administrator must configure the connection via the ASM **[Preferences]** > **[Directory Sync]** menu.
3. **IdP Authorization**: The administrator must log into the Identity Provider's portal (e.g., Google Admin Console or Azure Portal) to explicitly authorize the Apple app to access basic user directory data.
4. **Conflict Resolution**: If staff members have previously used their school email address to create a _personal_ Apple Account, Apple will provide a 60-day grace period for them to migrate to a private email address before the school claims total ownership of the domain.

## Operational Limitations:

- **Single Provider**: A domain can only be federated with one identity provider at a time (either Google OR Microsoft, not both).
- **Network Dependency**: Because authentication requests are routed to Google or Microsoft servers, an internet outage may temporarily prevent initial logins to Managed Apple Accounts.
- **Role Assignment**: Even with Federated Authentication enabled, administrators must still assign proper "Roles" (e.g., Instructor, Student, or Content Manager) within ASM to ensure users have the correct permissions within the Apple ecosystem.

## Strategy:

Implementing Federated Authentication significantly reduces ongoing administrative burdens (such as password reset requests) and ensures a sustainable, scalable IT architecture for the future.
