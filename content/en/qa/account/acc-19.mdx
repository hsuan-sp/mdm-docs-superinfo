**IT Administrators can precisely control AI feature availability through a dual-layered approach: 'Account-Level' configurations in Apple School Manager (ASM) and 'Device-Level' restrictions in the MDM.**

As Apple deeply integrated **Apple Intelligence** into the OS layer throughout 2024 and 2025, defining the boundaries of Generative AI in education became critical. Apple provides strict isolation mechanisms specifically designed for K-12 and Higher Education environments.

## The Dual-Layered Control Mechanism:

### 1. Account-Level Control (via ASM)

If your goal is to completely sever a student's ability to utilize Apple's cloud-based AI processing, you must configure this in ASM:

- **Path**: Log in to ASM > Click your name at the bottom left for **[Preferences]** > **[Service Access]**.
- **Function**: Within this interface, you can toggle Apple Intelligence on or off based on specific "Roles" (e.g., Students, Instructors, Staff).
- **The Advantage**: If disabled in ASM, even if a student signs into their Managed Apple Account on an **unmanaged, personally owned iPad or Mac**, that specific account remains explicitly barred from utilizing Apple's Private Cloud Compute resources. This guarantees institutional data control.

### 2. Device-Level Control (via MDM Profiles)

If you only wish to restrict institutional hardware from using specific AI features, you configure this in Jamf Pro:

- **Path**: Jamf Pro > **[Configuration Profiles]** > **[Restrictions]**.
- **Granular Control**: You can selectively disable distinct features like "Writing Tools," "Image Playground," or "Genmoji" without crippling standard Siri functions on the device.

## Strategy: AI Usage Policies

The responsibility for defining campus AI policies rests with local school boards.
We strongly recommend an "Opt-out by default" strategy during the initial adoption phase: Navigate to "Service Access" in ASM and globally disable Apple Intelligence for the "Student" role, while enabling it for "Instructors". Once your academic committee establishes clear guidelines regarding AI-assisted writing and academic integrity, you can progressively unlock specific features via MDM payloads for designated grade levels.
