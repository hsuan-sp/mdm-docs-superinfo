**Yes, this is critically important and must be addressed immediately.**

When Apple updates its service terms, a "Terms and Conditions" window will appear upon logging into Apple School Manager (ASM). IT leads must actively maintain access to this platform to ensure device management and app distribution are never interrupted by unaccepted terms.

## The Impact of Ignoring the Terms:

- **Registration Blocked**: New devices will fail the Automated Device Enrollment (ADE) process. Apple’s servers will reject registration requests until the terms are accepted.
- **VPP Sync Interruption**: The synchronization of Volume Purchase Program (VPP) content will pause. You will be unable to push new apps or update existing ones via your MDM.
- **Feature Lockout**: You may lose access to newer administrative capabilities, such as Apple Intelligence controls or viewing AppleCare coverage data.

## Who Can Accept the Terms?

Only users holding the **"Administrator"** role in ASM can accept these terms. "Content Managers" or "Site Managers" will see the notification but lack the authority to clear it.

## Practical Steps:

1. Log in to [school.apple.com](https://school.apple.com) using your Administrator account.
2. Follow the prompts in the banner or popup window.
3. Check all the **[I have read and agree...]** boxes.
4. Click **[Agree]**.

## Strategy:

Verify that your school has at least **two separate Administrator accounts** (e.g., the IT Director and a secondary systems admin). This redundancy ensures that if Apple updates their terms while the primary contact is on leave, global management of your fleet will not be paralyzed.
