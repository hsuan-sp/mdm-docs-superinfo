**This is a major system-level migration.**

Because the format of a Managed Apple Account is tied directly to the domain, any change to the email domain directly impacts user login identities and authentication flows.

## Pre-Migration Assessment:

- **Execution Timing**: It is strongly advised to perform this operation _only_ during **long summer or winter breaks**. Once the change takes effect, old accounts on all devices become invalid instantly, forcing a campus-wide re-authentication process.
- **Data Continuity**: The underlying "Internal ID" of the Apple Account remains unchanged. Therefore, cloud data (iCloud Drive files, Notes, Photos) migrates seamlessly with the new account name. However, users must "Sign Out and Sign Back In" to regain access to that data.

## Migration Procedure:

### Step 1: Add and Verify the New Domain

1. Log in to Apple School Manager ([school.apple.com](https://school.apple.com)).
2. Navigate to **[Preferences]** > **[Directory Sync]** (or Accounts).
3. Under the Domains section, click **[Add]** and input the new domain (e.g., `@school.xh.edu.tw`).
4. Follow the prompts to add the required TXT record to your public DNS. The domain becomes available for use only after Apple verifies this record.

### Step 2: Batch Update Account Formats

- **For Manually Managed Accounts (CSV)**:
  1. Export the entire user list as a CSV file from the **[Users]** section.
  2. Use the "Find & Replace" function in Excel to change the domain portion of the "Managed Apple Account" column to the new domain.
  3. Re-upload the modified CSV into ASM to update the accounts.

- **For Federated Authentication (Google / Microsoft)**:
  1. **Order of Operations**: You must complete the email domain migration on the Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra ID side **first**.
  2. Return to ASM and trigger a directory re-sync.
  3. ASM will detect the email address modifications and automatically update the corresponding Managed Apple Accounts.

### Step 3: Client-Side Action (Forced Re-login)

- Following the update, iPads and Macs will display "Account Verification Failed" prompts.
- **Required Action**: Users must manually sign out of the old domain account in Settings, and then sign back in using the **new email format**.

## Important Notes & Advice:

- **Federation Conflicts**: If the new domain was previously used by staff to create personal Apple Accounts, enabling federation triggers a **60-day conflict resolution process**. Affected users will be forced by Apple to change their personal account emails to private ones (e.g., @gmail.com).
- **VPP License Integrity**: App licenses are tied to the immutable internal ID; changing the domain will **not** result in lost App licenses.
- **Pre-Migration Testing**: Before rolling out a domain change campus-wide, test the migration on 2-3 IT staff accounts first to verify iCloud data continuity and MDM distribution remain intact.
