**Managed Apple Accounts in educational environments default to a generous 200GB iCloud storage quota. However, IT administrators "CANNOT" purchase additional storage tiers or expand this quota for individual users under any circumstances.**

This is a fundamental architectural difference compared to consumer Apple Accounts (where users can simply pay to upgrade to 2TB). In ASM, storage is treated as a provisioned organizational resource, not a personal hard drive.

## Strict Quota Specifications:

- **Education Organizations (ASM)**: Apple provides **200GB** of iCloud storage for every provisioned user (staff and students) entirely free of charge.
- **Business Organizations (ABM)**: The default is a mere 5GB. Organizations subscribing to Apple Business Essentials (ABE) can access 50GB or 200GB tiers. _(Note: As of 2026, ABE remains unavailable in many regions including Taiwan, leaving most local corporate managed accounts capped at 5GB)._

## Standard Operating Procedure for Exhausted 200GB Quotas:

Students typically exhaust their 200GB by indiscriminately backing up 4K video projects or massive photo libraries. Since you cannot buy your way out of this problem, IT staff must guide users through these steps:

1. **Offload Assignments via the 'Schoolwork' App**
   - Files and projects (Pages, Keynote, media) submitted natively through the Apple **Schoolwork** app utilize a separate, institutional storage mechanism. They do **not** count against the student’s personal 200GB iCloud quota. Encourage teachers to use this workflow.
2. **Purge Bloated System Backups**
   - Guide the student to **[Settings]** > **[Apple Account]** > **[iCloud]** > **[iCloud Backup]**.
   - Select the current device and manually toggle off the backup switches for non-essential applications or massive games that do not require cloud state saving.
3. **Leverage Secondary Institutional Cloud Storage**
   - Establish good data hygiene: Teach students to export and upload massive, static files (like finalized video projects) to the school's Google Workspace (Google Drive) or Microsoft 365 (OneDrive) environments. iCloud should be fiercely protected and reserved strictly for OS backups, app sync, and active lightweight documents.

## Strategy: Graduation Cycles and Data Transfer

During graduation cycles or when students transition to different programs, data management becomes critical.

- **Proactive Education**: IT Directors must actively promote "Digital Decluttering." Students must be taught to export critical portfolios to personal external drives or personal clouds before the academic year ends.
- **Strict Offboarding Policies**: Once a student graduates, their Managed Apple Account is deleted by the school, instantly vaporizing the contents of that 200GB iCloud drive. School IT will not have the bandwidth to field parent complaints about lost data. Establish and clearly communicate rigid deadlines for account deactivation and data purging.
