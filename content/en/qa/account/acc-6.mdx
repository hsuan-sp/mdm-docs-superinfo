**We strongly recommend 'Deactivating' the account first, and strictly proceeding to 'Delete' only after confirming the student has migrated necessary data. Please note that deletion is permanent and irreversible.**

Properly managing the lifecycle of a Managed Apple Account ensures that VPP App licenses are reclaimed and complies with educational data privacy and retention regulations.

## Recommended Workflow:

### Phase 1: Deactivate Account

1. Log in to [Apple School Manager](https://school.apple.com).
2. Click **[Users]** in the sidebar, then search for and select the student.
3. In the details panel on the right, click **[Change Account Status]**.
4. Change the status to **[Deactivated]**.

**Effects**:

- The student is immediately signed out of all devices and denied access to all Apple services.
- Data in iCloud (the 200 GB free tier) is retained on Apple's servers until the account is permanently deleted.
- Administrators can reactivate the account at any time.

### Phase 2: Retention Period

Retain deactivated accounts for 30 to 90 days post-graduation. Since Managed Apple Accounts cannot use the standard "Data and Privacy" page to download cloud backups easily, this grace period allows for temporary reactivation if a student needs to retrieve critical coursework.

### Phase 3: Delete Account

1. Select the deactivated user in ASM.
2. Click **[Delete User]** in the right-hand panel.

**Consequences**:

- All cloud data (Drive files, Notes, Photos) associated with the account is permanently wiped.
- Any VPP App licenses assigned to the user are automatically released back to the school's general pool.

## Batch Processing for Graduates:

1. In the **[Users]** list, use the filter tool to isolate a specific graduating class.
2. Select multiple students using keyboard shortcuts (hold **Command** or **Ctrl**, or use **Shift** for a continuous block).
3. Click **[Change Account Status]** in the panel to deactivate the entire class simultaneously.

## Strategy:

IT administrators should perform periodic audits of their ASM directory. Cleaning out old, graduated accounts ensures accurate VPP license counts and compliance with data minimization policies. If using Federated Authentication, remember to deactivate the user in your Google/Microsoft admin console first.
