**When newly purchased Apple hardware fails to automatically populate in your Jamf Pro inventory, the root cause is almost always an expired 'Server Token' or devices lingering unassigned within Apple School Manager.**

The Server Token acts as the cryptographic bridge linking Apple School Manager (ASM) to your MDM (Jamf Pro) for hardware procurement (a workflow veteran IT staff know as the Device Enrollment Program or DEP). Unlike the VPP Token which handles software licenses, the ADE Server Token exclusively manages hardware serial numbers. **It is strictly valid for one year and requires manual annual renewal.**

## Core Troubleshooting Analysis:

1. **Token Expiration**: Once the token expires, Jamf Pro severs communication with Apple’s enrollment servers. It can neither fetch new purchase serials nor update the enrollment flags (like Activation Lock status) of existing inventory.
2. **Unassigned Devices in ASM**: When a reseller submits your purchase to ASM, the devices initially land in an "Unassigned" pool. They must be explicitly assigned to your configured "MDM Server" within ASM before Jamf Pro is permitted to see them.
3. **Unaccepted Terms of Service**: As detailed in `acc-1`, if Apple publishes updated Terms and Conditions, all Automated Device Enrollment (ADE) synchronization is suspended at the server level until an Administrator accepts them.

## Standard Operating Procedure for Token Renewal:

1. **Download New Token**: Log in to [school.apple.com](https://school.apple.com) using an Administrator account.
2. Click your account name at the bottom left and navigate to **[Preferences]** > **[Your MDM Servers]**.
3. Select the active MDM server profile and click **[Download Token]** to save the file locally.
4. **Upload to Jamf Pro**: Log into your Jamf Pro dashboard. Navigate to **[Settings]** > **[Global Management]** > **[Automated Device Enrollment]** (or Device Enrollment Program).
5. Select your specific server instance, click **[Edit]**, upload the newly downloaded token file, and click **[Save]**.

## Synchronization Mechanics Context:

- **Force Sync / Sync Now**: If you just assigned a batch of devices in ASM and need them to appear in Jamf Pro immediately for profiling, click the **[Force Sync]** or **[Sync Now]** button in the Jamf Pro ADE interface.
- **Modern Automation Trends**: Apple has heavily optimized server-to-server webhook communications. Today, devices assigned in ASM typically sync to the MDM automatically within minutes. However, when onboarding large fleets (e.g., hundreds of iPads at once), manually triggering a sync remains the most reliable diagnostic step.

## Strategy: Automated Operations

- **Enable Default Assignment**: We strongly advise configuring "Default Device Assignment" within ASM settings. Once configured, any iPad or Mac purchased through an authorized reseller is automatically routed directly to your Jamf Pro server. This achieves true "Zero-Touch Deployment"—meaning IT staff only need to remember to renew the Server Token once a year, and the entire hardware intake pipeline becomes fully automated.
