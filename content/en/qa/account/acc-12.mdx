**While recent OS updates support 'Automated MDM Migration' without data loss, executing a silent, non-wipe migration is 'strongly discouraged' within the complex configurations of educational environments.**

When schools transition from a legacy MDM to a newly procured system, Apple provides the `MigrateDevice` command in **iPadOS 26 and macOS 26 Tahoe** to allow for wipe-free MDM swapping. However, this approach carries substantial operational risks in educational settings.

## Why 'Wipe-Free' Migration is Risky in Education:

| Risk Factor                       | Detailed Explanation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :-------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Profile Residue & Conflicts**   | "Restriction Profiles," "Wi-Fi Certificates," or "Education Configurations" deployed by the old MDM can stubbornly remain in the system registry. When the new MDM pushes its own payloads, conflicts arise that often break critical services like Apple Classroom or secure Wi-Fi authentication. |
| **Data Integrity & System Bloat** | After 4 years of intense student usage, devices accumulate massive amounts of cache, corrupted temporary files, and obsolete app data. Transitioning without a factory reset prevents the new deployment from achieving optimal performance.                                                        |
| **VPP License Synchronization**   | Handing over Volume Purchase Program (VPP) app licenses between two active MDMs without wiping the app can lead to orphaned licenses, preventing the new MDM from executing future app updates.                                                                                                     |

## The Best Practice Solution: Return to Service (RTS)

To guarantee a stable and clean foundation for your fleet during a transition, IT administrators should prioritize the **Return to Service (RTS)** framework for the migration.

**How RTS Works and Its Advantages:**

1. **Zero-Touch Erase and Connect**: When the administrator sends the RTS wipe command from the legacy MDM, it packages the school's Wi-Fi payload alongside the erase instruction.
2. **Fully Automated Provisioning**: Upon rebooting, the device automatically connects to the Wi-Fi, silently skips all Setup Assistant screens (Language, Region, etc.), and immediately enrolls into the **new MDM server** (as dictated by your ASM token assignment).
3. **The OS 26 Evolution**: In **iPadOS 26** and **macOS 26 Tahoe**, RTS received a massive upgrade. Administrators can now instruct the RTS process to **"Retain installed Managed Apps"** during the wipe. This achieves the holy grail of migrations: a perfectly clean OS state without the bandwidth-crushing necessity of re-downloading gigabytes of educational software.
