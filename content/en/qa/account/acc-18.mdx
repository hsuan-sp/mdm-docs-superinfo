**You absolutely do not need to repurchase any apps. All App licenses acquired through the Volume Purchase Program (VPP)—whether free or paid—are permanently bound to the 'Location' within Apple School Manager (ASM), completely independent of any specific MDM software.**

As long as your licenses reside in your ASM portal, IT administrators have full authority to unbind them from a legacy MDM (like a vendor-provided Jamf Pro instance) and reassign them to a newly procured MDM system.

## The Standard, Safe License Transfer SOP:

Directly uploading an active VPP Token to a new MDM while it is still actively assigning licenses in the old MDM will trigger severe synchronization conflicts. Follow this strict 3-step protocol:

1. **Step 1: Aggressively Reclaim Licenses in the Old MDM**
   - This is the most critical, yet frequently missed, step. Before decommissioning the legacy MDM, administrators must delete all app deployment scopes (e.g., Smart Group assignments) or manually trigger a "Revoke All" command.
   - **Verification**: Check the VPP dashboard in the old MDM. All app counts must return to "100% Available", indicating that the licenses have been successfully released back to ASM.
2. **Step 2: Transfer the VPP Token**
   - Log into [Apple School Manager](https://school.apple.com) and download a fresh `.vpptoken` file for the relevant Location.
   - Log into your new MDM platform and upload the token. This establishes the new cryptographic tunnel between ASM and your new server.
3. **Step 3: Trigger Content Synchronization**
   - Click **[Sync]** in the new MDM. It will instantly download your entire app inventory and available license counts from ASM, ready for you to build new deployment profiles.

## Advanced Technology: OS 26 Silent Migration

Historically, changing MDMs meant deleting all apps from iPads and forcing the new MDM to re-download them—a catastrophic event for school network bandwidth.
If both your legacy and new MDM providers support Apple's Migration APIs (introduced in iOS 17.5 and highly optimized in **OS 26**), you can execute a **"Silent Migration."**
Under a silent migration, the gigabytes of educational apps already installed on the devices **are not deleted**. Apple's backend seamlessly transfers the "Management Ownership" of those apps from the old server to the new server in the background. The transition is completely invisible to students and generates zero bandwidth load.

## Strategy: Vendor Decommissioning

If your school opts not to renew a vendor's MDM contract, you **must perform "Step 1: Reclaim Licenses" during the final week before the vendor deactivates the server.** If the old server goes offline while still hoarding active licenses, those licenses become "orphaned." Resolving orphaned licenses requires escalating a support ticket to Apple Engineering to force a backend release, which can take weeks and severely delay back-to-school app deployments.
