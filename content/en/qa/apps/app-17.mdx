**This is typically caused by a pending background synchronization between the 'Apple Server (ASM)' and your 'MDM Server (Jamf Pro)'.**

## System Mechanics

To conserve server resources, Jamf Pro defaults to syncing with ASM only once per day (or based on an admin-defined schedule). Therefore, when you click "Get" in ASM, the new VPP license data is not pushed "instantly" to Jamf Pro.

## Solution (Manual Sync)

If you need to distribute the app immediately, trigger a manual sync using these steps:

1. Log in to the Jamf Pro admin console.
2. Navigate to **[Settings]** > **[Global]** > **[Volume Purchasing]**.
3. Select the specific "Location" associated with the app purchase.
4. Switch to the **[Content]** tab and click the **[Force Update]** or **[Update]** button at the bottom.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes, refresh the page, and the newly purchased app should appear in your catalog.

## Checkpoint:

If it still doesn't appear after a forced sync, double-check that you selected the correct **Location** in ASM during purchase. A purchase made for "Campus A" will not be visible to an MDM server linked to "Campus B."
