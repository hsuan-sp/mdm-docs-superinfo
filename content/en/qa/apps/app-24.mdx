**The Declarative Device Management (DDM) framework in iOS/iPadOS introduces a new "Restrict App Download Over Cellular" configuration payload. This allows IT administrators to strictly enforce that student devices only execute app downloads and updates while connected to Wi-Fi, effectively preventing the exhaustion of SIM card cellular data.**

## Configuration

This feature is implemented via **DDM** configurations. In **Jamf Pro**:

### Jamf Pro Configuration Steps

1. In the Jamf Pro console, navigate to the **[Blueprints]** or **[App Declarations]** section.
2. Define the configuration for the specific managed apps.
3. Configure the **"CellularDataPolicy"** parameter within the deployment declaration:
   - **Disallow**: Strictly enforces downloading and updating over Wi-Fi only.
   - **Allow**: Permits installation over any available network, including cellular data.
4. Save and assign the declaration to your target device groups (devices must be Supervised and running a compatible OS).

## Use Cases and Benefits

- **Subsidized SIM Cards**: Many educational projects provide iPads or Chromebooks with built-in SIM cards and strict monthly data caps (e.g., 10GB/month). This restriction prevents students from accidentally draining their monthly data allowance by downloading large games or apps while off-campus.
- **BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) Management**: For personal student devices where parents cover the cellular data costs, this setting helps the school govern inappropriate download behaviors, preventing unexpected data overage charges and subsequent disputes.

## Impact and Limitations

- **Applies to**:
  - Manual App Store downloads.
  - MDM-initiated (DDM) automated installs.
  - App auto-updates.

- **Does NOT apply to**:
  - General web browsing or video streaming (YouTube still consumes data unless managed by a Content Filter).
  - In-app content updates (e.g., additional data packs inside a game).

- **User Experience**: When a student tries to download an app on 4G/5G, the button will be grayed out or a prompt will appear: "Please connect to Wi-Fi to download."

## Expert Strategy

Restricting app downloads is only one part of data management. For complete control, combine this with:

- **Network Content Filtering**: Deploy a Web Content Filter profile via MDM to restrict access to high-bandwidth streaming platforms (like YouTube or TikTok) over cellular.
- **Per-App VPN**: Force the network traffic of specific educational apps through the school's internal VPN server, ensuring they can only connect when interacting with the campus network environment.
- **Digital Literacy Education**: Clearly instruct students during early IT classes to identify and avoid digital behaviors that consume excessive bandwidth.
