**Due to regional licensing restrictions (including Taiwan), Apple School Manager (ASM) does not currently support the bulk procurement of content from the Apple Books Store.**

This means you cannot "purchase" paid or free books through ASM in the same way you acquire apps. However, if your teaching staff has created custom materials or legally acquired digital textbooks (PDF or ePub files), there are three effective distribution methods:

## Option 1: Native MDM Deployment (For lightweight, mandatory texts)

1. **Upload to Jamf Pro**: Navigate to the **[eBooks]** section within Jamf Pro. Here, you can directly upload your PDF or ePub files.
2. **Scope the Deployment**: Assign these custom e-books to specific Smart Groups (e.g., "All 7th Grade iPads").
3. **Student Experience**: The files are silently pushed. When students open the native Apple **Books** app, the school-provided materials will be resting on their digital bookshelves.

## Option 2: On-Demand via "Self Service" (Highly Recommended)

- **The Mechanics**: Instead of setting the eBook to "Install Automatically," publish it to **[Make Available in Self Service]**.
- **The Advantage**: This prevents 500 iPads from simultaneously downloading a massive file and consuming local storage. Students actively download the syllabus or reading material only when instructed by their teacher during class.

> **Storage Management Tip**: The 64 GB iPad's local storage is finite. Avoid using MDM to forcefully push large, multimedia-rich ePub files to every device. Adopting an on-demand download model (Option 2) or a cloud-based approach (Option 3) is the core principle for keeping the system lean and preventing app update failures caused by insufficient storage.

## Option 3: Cloud Storage or LMS Platforms (The Most Flexible Long-Term Approach)

- **The Mechanics**: Upload materials to a Learning Management System (LMS) like Canvas, Google Classroom, or a shared Google Drive folder. The MDM's only job is to deploy the LMS app or a Web Clip shortcut.
- **The Advantage**: The files live centrally in the cloud. When a teacher updates a typo in the PDF, every student instantly sees the correction without requiring the MDM to push a new 50MB payload.
