**Yes. By deploying a 'Home Screen Layout' Configuration Profile, administrators can enforce the exact placement of apps and folders on the iPad Home Screen.**

## Configuration Steps

1. In the Jamf Pro sidebar, navigate to **[Devices]** > **[Configuration Profiles]**.
2. Create a new profile and select the **[Home Screen Layout]** payload.
3. You can visually define the contents of the **[Dock]** and individual **[Pages]**.
4. The system supports creating folders to group similar apps together (e.g., iWork productivity apps).

## Important Limitations and Best Practices

- **Lockdown Effect**: Once applied, **students cannot move or delete app icons on the Home Screen**. The entire interface is "frozen" in a read-only state defined by the administrator.
- **Displacement**: Any installed apps not explicitly included in your layout will be pushed to the last page or hidden within the App Library.
- **Classroom Advice**: This feature is highly recommended for younger students or special education needs (simplifying the interface and preventing accidental deletion). For older students, we recommend maintaining flexibility so they can build their personalized digital learning environment.
- **Note**: Many regional MOE/School District setups already include a default layout. Verify yours before creating a competing profile.
