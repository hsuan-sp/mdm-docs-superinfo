**The key is to disable App Store 'Automatic Updates' and adopt a 'Maintenance Window' deployment strategy.**

Because background update times for the App Store cannot be precisely predicted, we recommend the following strategy for critical educational apps to ensure classroom stability:

## 1. Disable Automatic Updates per App

- In the App details page within Jamf Pro, **uncheck [Automatically update app]**.
- It is crucial to do this for core apps like Google Classroom, Meet, or exam-taking software, preventing potential bugs in new versions from disrupting lessons.

## 2. Establish a 'Maintenance Window'

- Agree on a fixed weekly maintenance window with the school administration (e.g., after 5 PM on Fridays).
- Use the **[Mass Actions]** feature in Jamf Pro to select the target device group and manually send the **[Update Application Version]** command.

## 3. Leverage Version Pinning

If your environment uses **Volume Purchase (VPP)**, while you cannot technically "downgrade" an app, you can choose not to trigger the update command. This keeps all devices on a stable, current version until a planned upgrade during breaks.
