Purchasing the wrong app version is a common mistake in educational licensing. For instance, Procreate (iPad) and Procreate Pocket (iPhone) are entirely separate product lines and do not support Apple's "Universal Purchase" feature.

## Product Specifications Comparison

- **Procreate (iPad version)**: Designed specifically for iPadOS and deeply integrated with the Apple Pencil. It supports large canvas sizes and a full rendering engine, making it the standard software for art programs. **It carries a higher unit price.**
- **Procreate Pocket (iPhone version)**: Designed for the smaller iOS interface. While it can run on an iPad via compatibility mode, it will display with black borders and lacks support for the Apple Pencil's advanced pressure and tilt sensitivity. **It has a lower unit price.**

## Standard Remediation Workflow for Incorrect Purchases

1. **Submit a VPP Refund Request**:
   - The IT administrator must log in to **Apple School Manager (ASM)**, navigate to **[Preferences]** > **[Payments and Billing]** > **[View Purchase History]**.
   - Alternatively, log in directly to **reportaproblem.apple.com** using the ASM Managed Apple Account.
   - Locate the incorrect order, select "Request a refund," and explicitly state "Purchased wrong version" in the reasoning field.
   - **Note**: Apple's review process for refund requests typically takes 24 to 48 hours, and submissions cannot be withdrawn.

2. **Repurchase the Correct Version**:
   - Because the licenses are not interchangeable, you must purchase the correct iPad version licenses in ASM before you can proceed with deployment.

3. **Revoke the Incorrect Scope**:
   - Log in to Jamf Pro and immediately remove the deployment scope for the "Pocket" version. This is critical to prevent the iPhone app from being mistakenly pushed to student iPads, which would negatively impact their learning experience.
