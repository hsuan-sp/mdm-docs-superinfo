**Absolutely not. As long as your deployment architecture rigidly adheres to "Device-based Assignment," the entire app lifecycle—from initial installation to every subsequent version upgrade—can be completely silent and require zero human intervention.**

Teachers frequently complaining about "password prompt bombardments" during app updates are almost always suffering from legacy "User-based Assignment" configurations or situations where students manually downloaded apps from the consumer App Store.

## The 2026 Technical Standard: Declarative Device Management (DDM)

As Apple's OS architecture evolves, **Declarative Device Management (DDM)** has entirely superseded traditional MDM push commands as the ultimate mechanism for silent updates:

- **Device Autonomy**: In the legacy MDM era, Jamf Pro had to actively push an "update now" command to 500 individual iPads. Under DDM, the server simply publishes a "Declaration." The iPad proactively monitors for new versions and autonomously decides to download and install the update in the background when the device is idle and adequately charged.
- **Granular Status Reporting**: Administrators now receive real-time, granular feedback (e.g., "Downloading 45%" or "Failed: Insufficient Storage") instead of the notoriously opaque "Pending" status of the past.

## Mandatory Prerequisites for Silent Updates:

1. **Supervised Mode**: The iPad must be cryptographically flagged as "Supervised." This is typically achieved automatically during the initial unboxing via Automated Device Enrollment (ADE).
2. **Strict Device-Based Assignment**: The VPP license must be bound exclusively to the hardware serial number.
3. **Auto-Update Toggle**: Within Jamf Pro's app configuration, ensure the **[Automatically Update App]** checkbox is enabled. On modern OS versions (iOS 17+), this checkbox seamlessly activates the DDM background update protocols.

## Troubleshooting: Why do updates fail despite correct settings?

If your configuration is flawless but the app remains stubbornly outdated, investigate these physical constraints:

- **The App is in the Foreground**: If a student is actively using the app, the OS will never force-quit it to apply an update, as this risks data corruption. The OS will wait until the app is backgrounded or the iPad goes to sleep.
- **Storage Exhaustion**: The OS requires temporary "swap space" to unpack the new binary while retaining the old one. If the 64GB iPad is choked with videos, the silent update process is aborted at the kernel level.
