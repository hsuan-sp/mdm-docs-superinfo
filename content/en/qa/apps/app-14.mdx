**Yes. Apple allows Managed Apple Accounts to be used as testers in TestFlight, but administrators must first enable the necessary permissions within Apple School Manager (ASM).**

## Prerequisite Check (ASM)

Administrators must log in to **Apple School Manager**, navigate to **[Settings]** > **[User Access & Directory Sync]** > **[Service Access]**, and verify that the **[TestFlight]** service is toggled to "On". If this is disabled, users will not be able to log in to TestFlight, even if they receive an invitation.

## Standard Deployment Workflow

1. **App Store Connect Setup**:
   - The school's development team must add the teacher's Managed Apple Account to an **External Testing** group in App Store Connect.
   - The system will automatically send an invitation email to that account.

2. **Distribute the TestFlight App**:
   - The IT administrator needs to procure free licenses for the **TestFlight** app via "Apps and Books" in ASM.
   - Use Jamf Pro to silently install TestFlight on the teacher's iPad using **Device-based Assignment**.

3. **Redeeming the Invitation**:
   - The teacher opens the invitation email on their iPad and taps "View in TestFlight".
   - Once TestFlight opens, the teacher logs in with their Managed Apple Account, accepts the invitation, and downloads the Beta app.

## Difference from Personal Accounts:

Managed Apple Accounts cannot perform In-App Purchases (IAP). Therefore, they cannot test Beta features involving real financial transactions unless conducted within a sandbox environment.
