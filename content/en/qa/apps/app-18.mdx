**A missing App Store icon is usually the result of a restriction imposed by local 'Screen Time' settings or an 'MDM Configuration Profile'. Please verify the following:**

## 1. Check Local "Screen Time" Settings (Most Common)

- **Path**: On the iPad, navigate to **[Settings]** > **[Screen Time]** > **[Content & Privacy Restrictions]** > **[iTunes & App Store Purchases]**.
- **Key Setting**: Check if **[Installing Apps]** is set to **"Don't Allow"**.
- **Explanation**: This is a local iOS restriction. Even if the MDM allows it, a user might have accidentally toggled this setting, causing the App Store to disappear.

## 2. Check MDM Restriction Profiles

- **Path**: On the iPad, go to **[Settings]** > **[General]** > **[VPN & Device Management]** and review the installed "Restrictions" profile.
- **Key Setting**: Look for an **"Allow installing apps"** restriction rule.
- **Explanation**: This MDM restriction applies specifically to "Supervised" devices. The administrator will need to remove this restriction payload from Jamf Pro.

## Step 3: Home Screen Search

Sometimes the icon is buried in a folder or has been "Removed from Home Screen" but remains in the App Library. Try pulling down on the Home Screen and searching for "**App Store**."

## Step 4: Reset Home Screen Layout (Last Resort)

If the icon is truly missing from the view, go to **Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPad > Reset > Reset Home Screen Layout**. (_Warning: This will undo all customized app groupings and folders._)
