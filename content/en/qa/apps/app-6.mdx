**It fundamentally depends on the "purchasing entity" and the "management payload." In IT asset management, the digital "App License" and the localized "App Data" must be evaluated separately.**

Within an educational deployment, applications are strictly bifurcated into "Managed" and "Personal" categories, each possessing drastically different lifecycles and data retention policies:

## 1. School-Assigned "Managed Apps"

- **Procurement Source**: Acquired by the IT Director via the Volume Purchase Program in **[Apple School Manager (ASM)]** and silently pushed to the device via the MDM server.
- **Asset Ownership**: The cryptographic license is the **exclusive property of the school**.
- **Graduation and Device Handover Protocols**:
  - **Instant License Revocation**: When a student graduates, or if an administrator issues an "Unenroll" or "Wipe" command via Jamf Pro, the MDM instantly revokes the app's license. The app will immediately vanish from the springboard. The license is seamlessly returned to the school's VPP pool for future cohorts.
  - **Catastrophic Data Loss Risk**: If the payload in Jamf Pro is configured with **[Remove app when MDM profile is removed]**, the OS will trigger a system-level purge. Any local data trapped inside the app's sandbox (e.g., un-synced GoodNotes files, half-finished iMovie projects) will be **permanently annihilated and cannot be recovered**.

## 2. Personal Apps Downloaded via a Consumer Apple Account

- **Procurement Source**: Apps downloaded by a student who navigated to the App Store and signed in with their private (e.g., `@gmail.com`) Apple Account.
- **Asset Ownership**: The license belongs permanently to the **individual student**.
- **Graduation Protocols**:
  - **Permanent Access**: The student retains lifelong access. They can redownload the app on any future personal device by authenticating with their private Apple Account, entirely outside the purview of the school's MDM.

## Critical Reminder: Data Export Before Device Resets

Any fleet reorganization, graduation cycle, or device reassignment that involves factory wipes will inevitably destroy local data:

- **Mandatory Data Export Campaigns**: IT Directors must issue clear instructions to teaching staff before each academic year ends. Students must export all portfolios, digital art, and notes as PDFs or MP4s to a cloud provider like Google Drive.
- **The Liability Shield**: Once a device wipe or reallocation sequence initiates, all Managed Apps and their siloed data will be permanently destroyed. IT staff bear no technical capability nor administrative obligation to recover lost student data.
