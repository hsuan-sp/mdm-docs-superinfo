**Yes, this is a mandatory requirement for official repairs. Failure to disable 'Find My' triggers 'Activation Lock,' which prevents authorized service centers from performing diagnostics or hardware replacement, resulting in the device being returned unrepaired.**

## Option 1: Admin Remote Unlock via Jamf Pro (Recommended)

If the device is **Supervised**, the IT administrator can handle this remotely via the management console:

1. Locate the specific device in Jamf Pro.
2. Send the **"Disable Activation Lock"** command.
3. **Effect**: This overrides the lock on Apple's servers for that specific serial number. It is the most reliable method when the device screen is unresponsive.

## Option 2: Manual Sign-out on the iPad

If the screen is functional and the student is present:

1. Go to **[Settings]** > **[User Name]** > **[Find My]**.
2. Tap **"Find My iPad"** and toggle it OFF.
3. The student must enter their Apple ID password to confirm.

## Option 3: Web-Based Remote Removal

If the screen is broken or the device will not power on:

1. Have the student log in at [iCloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find) using another device.
2. Select the specific iPad from the "All Devices" list.
3. Click **"Remove from Account"**.
4. **Warning**: Do _not_ select "Erase iPad," as this does not necessarily remove the activation lock in all scenarios and can complicate redeployment.

## Expert Strategy:

Before submitting a hardware repair batch, IT Coordinators should strictly verify the Activation Lock status of every unit in Jamf Pro. Incorporating a mandatory "Activation Lock Released" checkbox into your school's internal repair submission workflow prevents entire batches from being rejected and returned by the Apple Authorized Service Provider due to a single locked device.
