**iPadOS 26.5 and macOS 26.5 unlock support for 160 MHz bandwidth on compatible Wi-Fi 6E hardware, offering significantly higher peak wireless throughput.**

If your high-end devices aren't seeing speed improvements, it is typically due to Access Point (AP) configuration, frequency interference, or backbone network bottlenecks.

## Requirements for Maximum Speed:

### 1. Compatible Hardware & OS

- **Devices**:
  - iPad Pro 11"/13" (M2 or M4 chip models).
  - MacBook Pro (M2 Pro/Max or later).
  - Mac mini (M2 Pro or M4 or later).
- **Software**: Ensure devices are updated to **iPadOS 26.5** or **macOS 26.5** or later.

### 2. Access Point (AP) Configuration

- **Frequency**: Must support the **6 GHz (802.11ax)** band.
- **Channel Width**: The 6 GHz band must be manually configured to **160 MHz** (default is often 80 MHz).
- **Security**: **WPA3** encryption is mandatory for Wi-Fi 6E; older WPA2 protocols will prevent 6 GHz connectivity.

### 3. Wi-Fi 7 Outlook (2025+ Models)

- Newer **M5 chip models** (released late 2025) support Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be), which can reach 320 MHz bandwidth.
- **Note**: These speeds require a Wi-Fi 7 compatible router/AP infrastructure, which is still emerging in school environments.

## Common Troubleshooting Points:

- **Band Steering Issues**: If the school uses a single SSID for all bands, devices may fall back to 5 GHz as 6 GHz signals have shorter range. Consider setting a dedicated 6 GHz SSID (e.g., `School-6E`).
- **WPA3 Compatibility**: Some legacy RADIUS authentication servers do not support WPA3, causing devices to downgrade their connection.
- **Ethernet Uplink Bottleneck**: If the AP's uplink to the switch is only 1 Gbps, you will never see real-world speeds above that, regardless of the Wi-Fi handshake. Upgrading to **2.5 GbE or 10 GbE** switches is required for the full benefit.

## Expert Strategy:

When planning network infrastructure refreshes, schools should prioritize Wi-Fi 6E or Wi-Fi 7 access points to support bandwidth-heavy pedagogical tools like 4K streaming and AR/VR applications.
