**Newer iPads released in 2024–2025 feature significant hardware and management upgrades, specifically regarding Pencil compatibility, battery health, and Apple Intelligence support.**

## Core Hardware Comparison (2026 Fleet):

| Feature          | iPad 9th gen | iPad (A16, 10th gen) | iPad Air (M2)          | iPad Pro (M4)          |
| :--------------- | :----------- | :------------------- | :--------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Processor**    | A13 Bionic   | **A16 Bionic**       | **M2 Chip**            | **M4 Chip**            |
| **Screen**       | 10.2" LCD    | 10.9" LCD            | 11" / 13" LCD          | **Ultra Retina OLED**  |
| **Port**         | Lightning    | **USB-C**            | USB-C (10Gbps)         | **Thunderbolt 4**      |
| **Front Cam**    | Portrait Top | **Landscape Center** | **Landscape Center**   | **Landscape Center**   |
| **Apple Pencil** | 1st Gen      | **1st Gen / USB-C**  | **Pencil Pro / USB-C** | **Pencil Pro / USB-C** |

## Management and Hardware Upgrades:

### 1. Advanced Battery Health Management

- **Detailed Data**: Only on M2/M4/A17 Pro models can administrators see "Cycle Count" and "Maximum Capacity (%)" in settings.
- **80% Charge Limit**: Can be enforced via **Configuration Profiles**, significantly extending battery life for units stored in charging carts for long periods.

### 2. Apple Intelligence Support

- **Hardware Requirement**: Requires M-series or A17 Pro chips.
- **Admin Tip**: Use **Smart Groups** in Jamf Pro to target these specific models for AI-enhanced educational software deployments.

### 3. Apple Pencil Pro Tracking

- **Find My Support**: The Pencil Pro supports the "Find My" network. We recommend binding these to the teacher's **Managed Apple ID** to minimize loss during equipment transfers.

## Expert Strategy:

When procuring new hardware, schools should prioritize models with **Landscape Center** cameras for better video conferencing ergonomics. Additionally, ensure charging infrastructure is upgraded to support **30W+ USB-C PD** output to handle the faster charging capabilities of M-series iPads efficiently.
