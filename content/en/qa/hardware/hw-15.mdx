**Starting with macOS 15, Apple introduced a enhanced 'Media Management' framework for granular control over external storage. macOS 26 further strengthens these security controls.**

Managing USB access is vital for school computer labs and administrative offices to prevent unauthorized file transfers or malware injection.

## Important Background: macOS 26 and Intel Mac End-of-Life

- **Final Support**: macOS 26 is the **last supported version** for Intel-based Macs (announced WWDC 2025).
- **Eligible Models**: Limited to a few specific Intel Macs from 2019/2020.
- **Maintenance**: While security updates for macOS 26 will continue until 2028, macOS 27 (2026) will be Apple Silicon exclusive. Schools should prioritize upgrading to M-series Macs to maintain feature compatibility.

## Implementation Strategies in Jamf Pro:

### 1. Read-Only Mode

- Set external drives to read-only via a **Configuration Profile**.
- **Result**: Students can open teaching materials from a flash drive but **cannot copy sensitive files out** of the computer.

### 2. Full Block

- For exam environments or high-security administrative Macs, you can disable the mounting of external storage devices entirely.

### 3. Thunderbolt Accessory Security

- On Apple Silicon (M-series) Macs, administrators can enforce "User Approval" for data transfer when new accessories are connected, preventing automated "Rubber Ducky" style hardware attacks.
