**iPadOS 26 addresses the Bluetooth pairing issues common in Shared iPad environments. A new 'Audio Accessory Configuration' allows for 'Ephemeral Pairing' that does not sync to iCloud, solving the connection chaos in shared deployment scenarios.**

## The Problem (Prior to v26):

When Student A paired personal AirPods to a Shared iPad:

1. The pairing record was synchronized to their **Managed Apple ID**.
2. When Student B logged in later, the iPad might attempt to automatically connect to Student A's AirPods if they were still within range.
3. Student A might also find their AirPods constantly trying to reconnect to the school iPad after school hours.

## The iPadOS 26 Solution: Ephemeral Pairing

MDM can now push a specific configuration payload for audio accessories:

- **Function**: Allows pairing of AirPods or Beats but **blocks** writing that data to the iCloud Keychain.
- **Result**:
  - **Forget on Logout**: Once the student logs out of the Shared iPad, the device immediately "forgets" the headset.
  - **One-time Connection**: The headset does not "remember" the iPad as its primary owner, treating every session as a clean connection.

## Configuration Advice:

If your school provides headsets for language labs or permits students to use personal Bluetooth buds, enable this setting in Jamf Pro immediately. It will drastically reduce technical support tickets regarding audio cross-connectivity.

## Expert Strategy:

This feature requires compatible headset firmware. As campus wireless peripheral adoption grows, leveraging these automated clean-up protocols is the most effective way to achieve zero-touch accessory lifecycle management with minimal manual intervention.
