**AirDrop and AirPlay operate differently: AirDrop uses Peer-to-Peer communication, while AirPlay relies heavily on your campus network configuration and protocol pass-through.**

## AirDrop Troubleshooting (Point-to-Point):

1. **Interface State**: AirDrop requires both **Bluetooth** (for discovery) and **Wi-Fi** (for data transfer). Even without internet access, Wi-Fi must be toggled ON.
2. **Privacy Settings**: Since iPadOS 16.2, "Everyone" mode automatically reverts to "Contacts Only" after 10 minutes. Ensure the recipient has manually re-enabled "Everyone" for the transfer.
3. **MDM Restrictions**: Verify that the Jamf Pro **Restrictions** profile does not disable AirDrop. For **Supervised** devices, administrators can enforce these settings globally.
4. **Physical Interference**: In crowded classrooms, Bluetooth discovery can be inconsistent. Ensure devices are within 3 meters and have a clear line of sight.

## AirPlay Troubleshooting (Network-Based):

1. **VLAN Consistency**: This is the most common campus issue. If a teacher’s iPad is on the `Staff-Wi-Fi` network but the Apple TV is on the `Student-IoT` network, and those networks are isolated without proper routing, AirPlay packets cannot find their destination.
2. **Multicast/mDNS Filtering**: AirPlay uses the **Bonjour (mDNS)** protocol on **UDP port 5353**. If the school’s wireless access points have **"Client Isolation"** enabled, the Apple TV will not appear in the list.
3. **Network Optimization (mDNS Gateway)**: For complex campus networks with multiple VLANs, IT should enable an **mDNS Gateway (or Bonjour Proxy)** on the core switch or firewall to allow discovery packets to pass between subnets.
4. **Apple TV Security**: On the Apple TV, go to **[Settings]** > **[AirPlay and HomeKit]** and ensure "Access" is set to "Anyone on the Same Network."

## Expert Strategy:

When a single Wireless Access Point (AP) is subjected to high-density utilization (e.g., 30+ iPads actively communicating in one classroom), lower-tier APs will often drop low-priority mDNS multicast packets. This directly causes the AirPlay discovery list to appear delayed or completely empty. To ensure these advanced, network-dependent teaching functionalities operate without interruption, schools must continue to invest in and properly maintain a high-performance, enterprise-grade Wi-Fi infrastructure.
