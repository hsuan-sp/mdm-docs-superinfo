**Yes. Jamf Pro integrates with Apple's Global Service Exchange (GSX) database to automatically pull the "Limited Warranty" status for every managed device.**

However, IT administrators must understand a critical distinction: the warranty date displayed in Jamf Pro **only reflects Apple's factory records** and may differ significantly from your school's actual procurement contract coverage.

## How to View:

1. **Path**: Search for a specific device in Jamf Pro > **[Purchasing]** tab.
2. **Field Interpretation**:
   - **Warranty Expires**: The date Apple's official system shows the standard limited warranty ends (typically 1 year from activation).
   - **AppleCare ID**: Displays the contract number if the device is covered under AppleCare+, which also extends the warranty date automatically.

## ⚠️ The Warranty Blind Spot: Factory vs. Contract Coverage

Many schools have procurement contracts that include extended warranty coverage beyond Apple's standard 1-year factory warranty. If this extended coverage is provided via a **"Reseller Service Agreement"** rather than official AppleCare+:

- **Symptom**: Jamf Pro may report the warranty as "Expired," even though the device is still covered under your vendor's service contract.
- **Risk**: If IT staff rely solely on Jamf Pro's date, they may miss valid repair claims still covered by the procurement agreement.
- **Solution**: Use **Extension Attributes** in Jamf Pro to create a custom field titled "Procurement Contract End Date" and batch-upload the correct dates from your actual purchase agreement. This allows your MDM system to display both warranty timelines simultaneously.

## Expert Strategy: Proactive Warranty Management

1. **Advanced Search**: Create a search query for **"Procurement Contract End Date"** is **"Before [Date]"** to quickly identify devices about to lose contract coverage.
2. **Pre-Expiration Audit**: Before the contract expires, conduct a physical inspection of these devices (check for battery swelling, screen bright spots, etc.) to ensure all hardware issues are reported while still under coverage.
3. **Monthly Reporting**: In the final year of your contract, export warranty reports monthly. If you detect signs of batch-wide aging (e.g., declining battery health across a specific purchase lot), submit repair claims early to avoid costly out-of-pocket expenses after the contract ends.
