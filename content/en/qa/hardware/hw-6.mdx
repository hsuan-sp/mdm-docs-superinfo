**'Ghost Touch' (erratic touch input) typically involves Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) or a damaged digitizer layer. Auto-rotation issues are usually related to hardware failure of internal sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope).**

## Part 1: Troubleshooting Ghost Touch

### 1. Eliminate Electrical Noise (EMI)

- **Common Cause**: Unstable power from non-original cables or charging carts can interfere with the screen's capacitive sensors, causing erratic input.
- **The Test**: **Unplug all charging cables.** If the touch returns to normal on battery power, the issue is with your charging hardware. Switch to MFi-certified cables.

### 2. Physical Pressure & Cleaning

- **Case Pressure**: Verify that a rugged case is not pressing too tightly against the screen or that a screen protector is not peeling/cracked at the edges.
- **Cleaning**: Ensure the screen surface and edges are free from residue, oils, or liquid.

### 3. Force Restart

- **iPad 9th gen (with Home Button)**: Press and hold the Top button and the Home button until the Apple logo appears.
- **iPad (A16, 10th gen, without Home Button)**: Press and release Volume Up, then Volume Down, and then press and hold the Top button until the logo appears.

## Part 2: Determining Sensor Hardware Failure

If the screen fails to rotate, follow these steps:

1. **[Control Center] Lock**: Ensure "Rotation Lock" is not enabled in the Control Center.
2. **MDM Restriction Check**: Admins should verify that a **Restrictions** profile in Jamf Pro has not accidentally enabled "Lock Screen Orientation."
3. **Sensor Self-Diagnosis**:
   - **Test Method**: Open the built-in **Compass** app. If the needle is frozen or the leveling feature is unresponsive, the internal **Accelerometer** or **Gyroscope** hardware is likely faulty.

## Expert Strategy:

- **When to Repair**: If Ghost Touch persists after removing external power and a Force Restart, or if the Compass app remains unresponsive, the device requires official hardware service.
- **Warranty Coverage**: Provided there is no obvious physical damage or cracked glass, these issues are generally classified as natural hardware failures. Ensure you maintain an accurate audit of your fleet's warranty expiration dates and facilitate repairs before coverage lapses.
