**With the introduction of M4/M5 series iPads and Macs, school wireless environments are entering a 'Tri-generational' transition phase.**

Effective planning is required to ensure that high-performance devices aren't throttled by legacy network configurations while maintaining stable connectivity for older units like the iPad 9th gen.

## 2026 Campus Wireless Technology Comparison:

| Standard                | Bands           | Max Theoretical Speed | Example Devices                    |
| :---------------------- | :-------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)**  | 2.4 / 5 GHz     | 9.6 Gbps              | iPad 9th gen, iPad (A16)           |
| **Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax)** | **+ 6 GHz**     | 9.6 Gbps              | iPad Pro (M2/M4), MacBook Air (M3) |
| **Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)**  | 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz | **46 Gbps**           | iPad Pro (M5), iPhone 16           |

## Infrastructure Upgrade Planning Strategy:

### 1. Isolate the 6 GHz Band for Performance

Newer M-series devices support the 6 GHz band, which is free from interference by legacy appliances.

- **Recommendation**: Create a dedicated SSID for the 6 GHz band (e.g., `School-6G-Net`) for "Multimedia Labs" or "VR Classrooms" that require high bandwidth and low latency.

### 2. Mandatory WPA3 Security

- **Requirement**: Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 standards **mandate** WPA3 encryption.
- **Legacy Management**: Older iPads may struggle with WPA3. Ensure that general-purpose SSIDs support WPA2/WPA3 Mixed Mode to maintain backwards compatibility.

### 3. Benefits of Multi-Link Operation (MLO)

Wi-Fi 7 introduces **MLO**, allowing M5 chip devices to transmit data across multiple bands simultaneously.

- **Result**: Even if interference occurs on one band, the instructional stream continues uninterrupted on the other, ensuring smoother classroom video sessions.

### 4. Eliminating the Ethernet Bottleneck

When deploying Wi-Fi 7 Access Points, the core switches must support **Multi-Gig (2.5 GbE or 5 GbE)**. Plugging a high-performance AP into a standard 1 Gbps port creates an immediate bottleneck for high-density student traffic.

## Expert Strategy:

For standard classrooms, **Wi-Fi 6E** already provides excellent value. **Wi-Fi 7** is best prioritized for specialized creative labs or STEAM environments. IT leads should also verify that their PoE switches provide sufficient power (PoE+ or PoE++) to support the higher energy requirements of high-performance Wi-Fi 7 access points.
