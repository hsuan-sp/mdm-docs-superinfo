**"Resetting Network Settings" is a last-resort method to resolve stubborn connectivity issues. Administrators must exercise extreme caution when managing the transition of MDM Wi-Fi profiles to prevent devices from permanently dropping offline and becoming "Unmanaged."**

## ⚠️ High-Risk Operation Warning (Network Migration Pitfalls)

If your school is in the middle of a Wi-Fi infrastructure migration (e.g., transitioning from an old SSID to a new campus standard network), **absolutely do not delete the old Wi-Fi profile from the MDM first.**

- **The Standard Procedure**: Push the "New Wi-Fi Profile" via MDM $\rightarrow$ Verify devices successfully connect to the new network $\rightarrow$ Finally, remove the old Wi-Fi profile.
- **The Catastrophic Consequence**: If you hastily delete the old configuration or perform a "Reset Network Settings" on the device, the iPad will instantly lose its connection. Once offline, the device cannot receive the new Wi-Fi payload from Jamf Pro, forcing the IT team to **manually reconnect every single device by hand**—a disaster for large-scale deployments.

## When to Perform a Reset:

Use this only when the device shows "Unable to Join Network," fails to see any Bluetooth devices, or Jamf Trust is permanently stuck in a "Connecting" loop that a restart won't fix.

## Steps to Reset:

1. Navigate to **[Settings]** > **[General]** > **[Transfer or Reset iPad]** > **[Reset]**.
2. Select **[Reset Network Settings]**. The iPad will automatically reboot.
3. **Post-Reboot System Impact**: All manually saved Wi-Fi passwords and Bluetooth pairings will be entirely wiped. However, managed Wi-Fi profiles pushed by Jamf Pro should automatically re-apply once the device is unlocked and detects the campus network signal (provided the MDM profile was never revoked).

## Strategy: Practical Tip

Before resorting to a full network reset, always try toggling **Airplane Mode** for 10 seconds or performing a hard restart. These solve 90% of connectivity hang-ups without erasing your saved configurations.
