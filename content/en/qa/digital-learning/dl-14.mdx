**This is a common classroom management challenge. Beyond utilizing a 'Restrictions' profile to block local changes, you can use a 'Batch Renaming' tool to quickly standardize your entire fleet. Maintaining strict standardization and consistency in device naming is absolutely vital for effective whole-school asset management, auditing, and precise troubleshooting.**

## The Three-Step Solution:

### 1. Stop the Bleed: Disable Local Modification

- In the Jamf Pro **Restrictions** configuration profile, **uncheck** `Allow modifying device name`.
- **Result**: The "Name" field in **Settings > General > About** will turn gray and become unclickable, preventing students from altering the identity of the device.

### 2. The Treatment: Batch Rename with 'The MUT'

To correct hundreds of already-mislabeled iPads, use **The MUT (Mass Update Tool)** instead of manual editing.

- **Tool Info**: A free utility developed by Jamf staff. Download it from the **Mac App Store** on macOS.
- **Action**:
  1. Prepare a CSV with two columns: **Serial Number** and **Device Name**.
  2. Open The MUT, and log in with your Jamf Pro credentials.
  3. Upload the CSV and click **Update**.
  4. The system will instantly push renaming commands to all targeted devices.

### 3. Prevention: Enforce Naming

Enable the **Enforce Mobile Device Name** feature in Jamf Pro for your managed groups.

- **Result**: This background mechanism monitors the fleet. If a device's local name deviates from its database record, the MDM automatically pushes a correction command. This is the industry-standard automated approach for maintaining large-scale educational deployments.

## Strategy:

For any future device additions, define your naming convention (e.g., `Class-2026-%SERIALNUMBER%`) directly within the **PreStage Enrollment** settings. New devices will then be named automatically upon initial activation, requiring zero manual effort.
