**Smart Groups are the core automation engine within Jamf Pro for managing large fleets of educational devices.** They allow IT administrators to dynamically and automatically categorize devices based on precise conditions such as "Asset Tags," "OS Versions," or "Procurement Batches."

By utilizing Smart Groups, you can easily target specific batches of hardware (e.g., "2024 Intake" or "2025 Expansion") with highly tailored educational apps or restriction profiles without resorting to manual selection.

## Common Logic Examples:

### 1. Based on Naming Conventions (Recommended)

- If you named your new arrivals with a year prefix (e.g., `2025-iPad-001`).
- **Criterion**: Device Name **like** `2025-%`.
- **Result**: All devices from the 2025 intake are instantly grouped for easy management.

### 2. Based on Asset Tags

- If you uploaded a CSV of your asset tags into Jamf.
- **Criterion**: Asset Tag **is** `EDU-PROJ-A`.

### 3. Application Compliance Auditing

- **Criterion**: `Application Title` **[does not have]** `Jamf Trust` (or your chosen security app).
- **Purpose**: This creates a real-time audit list of every device that is missing its mandatory protection agent, providing the ICT team with an actionable daily troubleshooting queue.

## Strategy: Setup and Monitoring

Log in to the Jamf Pro console $\rightarrow$ **[Devices]** $\rightarrow$ **[Smart Device Groups]** $\rightarrow$ **[New]**.
After defining your criteria, strongly consider checking the box for **"Show in Jamf Pro Dashboard."** This provides a visual pie chart or numeric tile on your home screen, allowing you to instantly monitor the total count and health status of that specific device cohort.
