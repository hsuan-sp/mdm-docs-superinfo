**Utilize the 'Return to Service (RTS)' feature within the Apple MDM architecture for a seamless transition from 'Wipe' to 'Redeploy' without manual Wi-Fi configuration. This Zero-Touch automated workflow remains the absolute most efficient strategy for managing massive school IT asset turnovers and minimizing staff workload.**

End-of-year rotation is a critical task for IT staff. Follow this workflow to ensure devices are clean and ready for the next academic year:

## Standard Operating Procedure (SOP):

### 1. Data Export and Sign-Out (T-minus 2 weeks)

- Notify students to export personal projects and notes to Google Drive or external storage.
- If personal Apple Accounts were permitted, remind students to sign out to avoid potential Activation Lock issues.

### 2. Execute Return to Service (Recommended)

This method is significantly faster than a traditional wipe:

1. Select the devices in Jamf Pro.
2. Send the **Wipe Device** command.
3. **Crucial Step**: Check the **Return to Service** option in the command window and specify the school's Wi-Fi profile.
4. **Result**: The device will erase, **automatically reconnect to Wi-Fi**, skip initial setup screens, and land on the "Remote Management" screen awaiting the new student.

### 3. Hardware Inspection & Storage

- Check for physical damage like cracked screens or bent frames.
- Clean using 75% alcohol applied to a cloth.
- **Battery Care**: For long-term summer storage, keep battery levels at **50%-60%** and power down the device to prevent deep discharge.

### 4. Issuance to New Students

New students will see the "Remote Management" screen immediately upon powering on. They can complete enrollment (e.g., via student ID authentication) and automatically receive their required apps.
