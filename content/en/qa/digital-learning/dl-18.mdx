**No. For Supervised devices, MDM provides a 'Clear Passcode' command that removes the lock while keeping all student data intact. This is the most efficient rescue method, saving significant time compared to a full restoration.**

## Standard Rescue Steps (Jamf Pro):

1. **Verify Connectivity**: Ensure the device is powered on and connected to the internet (via Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
2. **Send Command**:
   - In Jamf Pro, go to the specific device record.
   - Click **Management > Remote Commands > Clear Passcode**.
3. **Result**:
   - Within seconds, the iPad screen will flicker or briefly show a spinning wheel.
   - The passcode is removed. You can swipe to enter the Home Screen immediately.
   - **Note**: Biometric data (Touch ID / Face ID) is also cleared as a security measure and must be re-registered by the user.

## What if the iPad is offline (e.g., Airplane Mode)?

If the device cannot reach the internet, the MDM command will stay "Pending" in the cloud. You can force a connection using a wired adapter:

- **Lightning Models (iPad 9)**: Use a "Lightning to Ethernet Adapter" (external power must be connected to the adapter).
- **USB-C Models (iPad 10/Air/Pro)**: Use a standard "USB-C to Ethernet Adapter."
- **Action**: Plug the adapter into the locked iPad and connect a network cable. The iPad will automatically fetch an IP via DHCP. Once online, the "Clear Passcode" command will execute instantly.

## Strategy: Final Resort

Only if both Wi-Fi and Ethernet connectivity are impossible should you resort to a full **Recovery Mode** restore via a computer. Be absolutely aware that hardware restoration will permanently eradicate any data that has not been backed up.
