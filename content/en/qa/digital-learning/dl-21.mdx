**Regardless of whether they were project-assigned or school-purchased, we strongly recommend maintaining the MDM license and management structure after the original hardware warranty or maintenance contract expires. Abandoning centralized management will cause devices to fall out of the security net and significantly increase the daily maintenance burden on IT staff.**

## Why Keep the MDM?

### 1. Security

Continued web filtering protects students from inappropriate content, and MDM-enforced restrictions prevent the installation of games or unauthorized apps.

### 2. Instructional Continuity

Maintain the ability to push apps, manage Wi-Fi settings, and support Apple Classroom.

### 3. Asset Integrity

MDM tracking helps locate misplaced devices and monitors battery health, ensuring the hardware lasts as long as possible.

## Strategy: Critical Management Tasks Before and After Warranty Expiration

### 1. Unified Health Checks

- **Battery Health**: Identify units needing battery replacement before they become unusable.
- **OS Updates**: Evaluate if the entire fleet should move to **iPadOS 26**.
- **Audit**: Record physical damage and update the school's overall asset registry.

### 2. Modernizing Use Case: Graduation and Rotation

When a student graduates and the device must be reassigned, use the **Return to Service** feature. This is the fastest way to reset a device running **iPadOS 26** or later.

**The Return to Service Advantage**:

- **Secure Wipe**: Erases all student data and browsing history.
- **App Preservation**: Unlike a traditional wipe, you can choose to keep Managed Apps on the device. This saves 50–70% of the total reset time and significantly reduces network bandwidth strain.
- **Auto-Reconnect**: The device remembers its Wi-Fi settings and re-enrolls automatically.

| Feature          | Traditional Erase All Content and Settings | Return to Service (iPadOS 26+) |
| :--------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| **Wipe Data**    | ✅                                         | ✅                             |
| **Keep Apps**    | ❌ (Redownload needed)                     | ✅ (Ready immediately)         |
| **Keep Network** | ❌ (Manual setup)                          | ✅ (Automatic)                 |
| **Time**         | 30–60 minutes                              | 5–15 minutes                   |

### 3. Transitioning to a Sustainable Usage Model

As you move into the daily maintenance and operational phase, consider a more flexible "Strategy" model for long-term fleet health:

- **Flexible Checkout**: Use iPad trolleys for specific projects rather than fixed 1-to-1 assignments.
- **Quality over Quantity**: Encourage deep integration in relevant subjects rather than forcing daily use in every class.

### 4. Technical Checklist

- **Stay Online**: Keep devices connected to Wi-Fi so the MDM can push critical security patches.
- **Backup Configs**: Export your current Jamf Pro configuration profiles as a backup.
- **Monitor Unmanaged Devices**: Use Smart Groups to catch devices that have been offline for over 60 days, as these are often tucked away in drawers and forgotten.
