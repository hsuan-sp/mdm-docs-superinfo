**This is a fundamental control for Supervised school-owned deployments. To avoid app ownership confusion, Activation Lock issues, and privacy risks, IT administrators must enforce a 'Restrictions' profile to fully lock account settings and App Store access. Maintaining school-issued devices strictly as "education-only" assets is the paramount directive for long-term fleet management.**

## Standard Protection Settings (Jamf Pro):

### 1. Lock Account Modification (Most Critical)

- In the **Restrictions > Functionality** section of your configuration profile.
- **Uncheck**: `Allow modifying account settings`.
- **Result**: The Apple Account section at the top of the iPad Settings menu will turn gray and become unclickable. Students will be unable to sign in, sign out, or modify any account details. This effectively prevents the device from being tied to a personal "Find My" lock.

### 2. Disable App Store

- **Uncheck**: `Allow installing apps using App Store`.
- **Result**: The App Store icon will disappear from the Home Screen entirely, cutting off the student's ability to download non-educational content.
- **Proper Deployment**: All required apps should be pushed via the MDM using **Volume Purchase (VPP)** with Device-based Assignment. This allows apps to install without requiring an Apple Account on the device.
