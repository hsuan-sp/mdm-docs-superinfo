**Consistent branding reinforces school identity and simplifies asset tracking. We recommend combining 'Automated Management' for silent image deployment and a 'Restrictions' profile for policy enforcement. Maintaining a highly unified aesthetic across the fleet provides a decisive advantage for visual asset identification and classroom management in large deployments.**

## Implementation Steps (Jamf Pro):

### 1. Automated Management (Recommended)

- **Scenario**: Use this for a "set and forget" approach where new devices receive the school wallpaper automatically upon enrollment.
- **Path**: Navigate to a **Smart Mobile Device Group > Automated Management** tab.
- **Action**: Click **Set Wallpaper**. Upload your image (JPG/PNG) and apply it to both the "Lock Screen" and "Home Screen."
- **Result**: Any device entering this group will have its wallpaper forcibly updated.

### 2. Manual Mass Action

- **Scenario**: Best for one-time events like a school anniversary.
- **Path**: In the device group view, click **Action > Send Remote Commands > Set Wallpaper**.

### 3. Critical Step: Locking the Permission

Without this restriction, students can easily revert to their personal photos.

- **Path**: Within a **Restrictions** configuration profile > **Functionality**.
- **Setting**: Uncheck **Allow modifying wallpaper**.
- **Effect**: The "Wallpaper" menu in iPad Settings will become grayed out and unclickable.

## Strategy: Dynamic Lock Screen Messages

Instead of manually editing text onto images, use the **Lock Screen Message** payload to display real-time asset data:

- **Settings**: Use variables like `Asset ID: $ASSET_TAG` or `Assigned to: $DEVICENAME`.
- **Effect**: The device pulls this info from the Jamf database and displays it at the bottom of the lock screen, making physical audits significantly faster.
